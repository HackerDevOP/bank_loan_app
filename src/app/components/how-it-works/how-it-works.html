Loan Lifecycle Explained

How Bank Loans Work: From Application to Payoff

A loan is an agreement where the lender provides capital upfront, and the borrower repays it over an agreed tenure along with interest, structured through scheduled installments.

01

Digital Application

Submit income proof, employment status, PAN verification, and liability records through the online portal.

02

Credit Evaluation

Underwriters analyze debt-to-income (DTI) ratio, credit scores, and financial history to determine interest rates.

03

Direct Disbursement

Upon officer sign-off and e-signature of loan terms, funds are wired directly to your designated bank account.

04

EMI Repayment

Repay via automated monthly installments split between principal balance and amortized interest.

Principal vs. Interest

The principal is the pure sum borrowed. Interest represents the lender's fee, calculated as a percentage (APR) of the outstanding principal balance.

Secured vs. Unsecured

Secured loans (mortgages, auto) require collateral backing for lower APRs. Unsecured loans (personal credit) rely purely on your credit score.

Amortization Schedule

Early monthly EMI payments primarily cover accrued interest charges, gradually shifting toward repaying the core principal balance over time.
